Welcome! This is the first post on my new blog, built with Astro and managed through Git.

## What I will write about

I am a technologist, so you will find articles about technology here. I also plan to share stories from the places I travel to, and lessons from everyday life.

## How this blog works

Every article is a simple Markdown file stored in a Git repository. When I publish a change, the site is rebuilt and deployed automatically.

### Available in more than one language

Each article can be written in multiple languages. Use the language button in the header to switch.

## Stay in touch

Thanks for reading. More posts are coming soon.

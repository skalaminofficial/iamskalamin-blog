## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device. Browsers also offer similar technology called local storage.

## Cookies on this website

This website does not set any cookies of its own, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Because of this, no cookie consent banner is shown.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may in some circumstances use strictly necessary security cookies to protect the website against abuse. These are not used for tracking or advertising.

## Local storage

The website stores the following items in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to a server.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| `theme` | Remembers your choice of light or dark theme | Until you clear it |
| `accent` | Remembers your chosen accent color | Until you clear it |

If you choose the "System" theme, the `theme` item is removed and your device setting is followed instead.

## Managing and clearing data

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time from your browser's privacy or site settings. The website continues to work normally afterwards, using default appearance settings.

## Future changes

If analytics, comments or other features that need cookies are added, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested where required by law.

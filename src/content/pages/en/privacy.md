Your privacy matters. This website is a personal blog designed to collect as little information as possible.

## In short

- There are no user accounts, sign-up forms or comment systems on this website.
- There is no advertising and no third-party tracking.
- Your theme, accent color and similar preferences are stored only on your own device.

## Information we collect

This website does not ask you for personal information, and we do not knowingly collect any directly. If you contact the author by email or through a social network, the message and your contact details are used only to reply to you.

## Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most web hosts, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the website, keep it secure and prevent abuse. This processing is governed by [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). The author does not use this data to identify visitors.

## Preferences stored on your device

To remember your choices, the website saves your selected theme (light, dark or system) and accent color in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device. See the Cookie Policy for details and how to clear it.

## Search

The search feature runs entirely in your browser. Your search terms are not sent to the author or to any third-party search service.

## Third-party links and sharing

Articles may link to other websites, and the share buttons open X, Facebook or LinkedIn only when you click them. Those services have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

## Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal profile about you, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. If you believe the author holds personal information about you, contact the author and it will be handled in line with applicable law.

## Changes to this policy

If features such as analytics or comments are added in the future, this policy will be updated before they go live. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact links provided in the footer of this website.
